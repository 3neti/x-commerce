# The Customer Purchases An Outcome

The customer is purchasing an outcome, not a software feature.

## Commercial Rule

Features describe capability. Outcomes describe the result a buyer is willing to fund.

For a rural bank, the purchased outcome may be the ability to offer digital transaction access without replacing its core banking system.

For a payroll sponsor, the purchased outcome may be completed disbursement to qualified beneficiaries.

For a government agency, the purchased outcome may be evidenced subsidy delivery.

For a merchant, the purchased outcome may be completed collection and settlement.

## Documentation Implication

Product documents should start with outcomes, then map to software, operations, legal dependencies, and pricing.
