# Why x-commerce Exists

x-commerce exists because execution alone is not a business.

x-change can represent, authorize, execute, complete, and evidence value-bearing instructions. That is necessary, but it does not answer how an outcome is packaged, priced, licensed, sold, supported, metered, commissioned, or shared among participants.

x-commerce fills that commercial architecture gap.

## Current Need

The proposed RBAP Digital Banking Program needs stakeholder-specific answers to:

```text
What is in this for me?
```

That answer requires more than product features. It requires a coherent model for:

- who owns intellectual property;
- who operates commercially;
- who contracts with rural banks;
- who pays provider costs;
- who earns markup;
- who receives royalties, commissions, or referral fees;
- who carries support obligations;
- who benefits from customer adoption;
- what happens if adoption is slower than expected.

## Boundary

x-commerce does not settle legal characterization. It records commercial assumptions and directs legal questions to x-legal.
