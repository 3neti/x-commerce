# Commercial Architecture

Commercial architecture explains how economic value moves through a system without confusing technical execution with business sustainability.

## Required Distinctions

- IP ownership;
- commercial operation;
- software licensing;
- implementation;
- hosting;
- transaction revenue;
- value-added service revenue;
- marketplace revenue;
- bank-generated revenue;
- provider costs;
- royalties;
- commissions;
- settlement and rail fees;
- pass-through funds.

## Commercial Chain

```text
3neti
    owns and develops the intellectual property
        |
ODTI
    licenses and commercially operates the technology
        |
Participating rural banks
    obtain licenses through ODTI
        |
Depositors, employers, remitters, merchants, government agencies,
and other customers consume rural-bank-branded digital services
```

This chain is a working premise and must not be represented as accepted by every named participant.
