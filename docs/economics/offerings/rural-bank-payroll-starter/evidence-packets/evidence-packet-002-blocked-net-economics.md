## Evidence Block A: Rural Bank Internal Support Cost

Target: `RB-002`

Required evidence:

- activities and roles attributable specifically to Payroll Starter;
- time per employer onboarding, active employer-month, payroll run, and exception event;
- loaded labor-cost or approved internal-rate basis;
- non-labor incremental operating cost;
- fixed and variable cost separation;
- actual, estimated, budgeted, and judgmental values identified separately;
- finance-approved allocation method and exclusions.

Minimum acceptable evidence: bank operations interview with activity categories, unit basis, estimate method, and finance review.

Preferred evidence: time-and-motion records, support tickets, staffing schedules, exception logs, reconciliation workload, and observed pilot activity.

## Evidence Block B: NetBank Or Infrastructure Role

Targets: `NET-001`, `NET-002`

Required evidence:

- factual role and regulated responsibility;
- account, API, rail, settlement, reconciliation, and support scope;
- setup, recurring, transaction, settlement, reconciliation, and support fees;
- successful-versus-attempted billing rule;
- reserves, minimums, and contract term;
- internal operating burden, if disclosed;
- contracting entity and customer-fund structure;
- gross-versus-net and internal-elimination implications.

Minimum acceptable evidence: written role response and fee proposal or explicit no-fee statement with validity period.

Preferred evidence: approved fee schedule or draft contract, API and settlement documentation, reconciliation sample, support scope, and reviewed accounting boundary.

## Evidence Block C: Collection And Cash Timing

Target: `COL-001`

Required evidence:

- invoice timing and payer;
- due dates and actual or expected payment lag;
- Rural Bank retention timing;
- ODTI, provider, cloud, and NetBank payment timing;
- treatment of late payment, non-payment, refunds, reversals, and disputed amounts;
- whether downstream payments wait for collection;
- working-capital responsibility;
- accounting recognition separated from cash receipt.

Minimum acceptable evidence: draft collection policy reviewed by Rural Bank and ODTI finance owners.

Preferred evidence: approved billing terms, comparable aging data, bank collection history, and accounting-reviewed cash-flow treatment.

## Evidence Block D: Tax And Withholding

Target: `TAX-001`

Required evidence:

- tax treatment by customer fee and stakeholder entity;
- VAT or other indirect tax treatment;
- withholding by payment type;
- cloud and foreign-provider treatment where relevant;
- royalty and related-party treatment;
- timing relative to billing, collection, accrual, and payment;
- documentation and filing responsibilities;
- explicit limitations and jurisdiction basis.

Minimum acceptable evidence: professional issue list and provisional treatment clearly marked non-final.

Preferred evidence: approved written tax treatment mapped to model line items and contract terms.

## Evidence Block E: 3neti Royalty Or License Basis

Target: `ROY-001`

Required evidence:

- fixed, variable, or other basis;
- gross or net base and exclusions;
- payment and collection timing;
- intellectual-property and license scope;
- related-party governance;
- legal characterization;
- accounting recognition;
- tax and withholding treatment;
- approval and review trigger.

Minimum acceptable evidence: draft decision paper with explicit unresolved professional-review items.

Preferred evidence: approved agreement and completed legal, accounting, tax, and related-party review.

## Evidence Block F: Capital-Budgeting Discount Rate

Target: `FIN-001`

Required evidence:

- capital-budgeting stakeholder and decision boundary;
- cash-flow currency, term, tax basis, and nominal or real basis;
- approved policy, hurdle rate, cost-of-capital analysis, or documented finance recommendation;
- permitted and prohibited use;
- owner, reviewer, approval status, effective date, and expiry;
- confirmation that the value was not selected to achieve a desired result.

Minimum acceptable evidence: finance recommendation with a documented basis and required cash-flow consistency.

Preferred evidence: approved capital-allocation policy or decision record with a review trigger.

## Provenance And Acceptance Gate

Every response must include the shared provenance block from [../evidence-instruments/README.md](../evidence-instruments/README.md).

Each assumption is reviewed independently. Packet completion requires:

1. a canonical assumption ID with no duplicate;
2. factual evidence, decision evidence, or professional review appropriate to the assumption;
3. respondent, owner, reviewer, permitted use, confidentiality, and validity period;
4. explicit range semantics where a range is supplied;
5. replacement or blocked-treatment recommendation;
6. downstream workbook and stakeholder-view impact;
7. unresolved dependencies left visible;
8. no direct workbook edit.

## Review Outcomes

Allowed outcomes:

- Evidence-supported Active candidate;
- Approved input candidate;
- Requires commercial decision;
- Requires legal, accounting, or tax review;
- Retain Blocked;
- Evidence unavailable or not disclosed;
- Out of current model boundary.
