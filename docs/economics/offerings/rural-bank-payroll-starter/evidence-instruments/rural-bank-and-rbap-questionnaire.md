## Payroll Portfolio

- How many active employer payroll clients does the bank currently serve?
- What employer segments are represented?
- What evidence supports the count?
- What is the approximate employee or recipient range per employer?
- How frequently do employer payroll runs occur?
- What current payroll transaction volume can be shared?
- What failed-payment, correction, reversal, or exception patterns occur?
- How is payroll currently reconciled?
- What current pricing or fee schedules can be shared, if any?
- Which data is actual portfolio evidence, management estimate, or interview-based?

Related assumptions: `CUS-001`, `CUS-002`, `CUS-003`, `VOL-002`, `CUS-006`.

## Adoption Readiness

- Is the bank willing to pilot the Payroll Starter offering?
- What internal approvals are required?
- What is the expected onboarding process?
- What technical readiness gaps exist?
- What compliance readiness gaps exist?
- What staffing readiness gaps exist?
- What support expectations would the bank have?
- What go-live timing is realistic after approval?
- What could delay activation?

Related assumptions: `ADP-001`, `ADP-002`, `ADP-003`, `RISK-001`, `OPS-005`.

## Commercial Appetite

Do not ask the respondent to approve existing working assumptions. Ask for evidence, ranges, and constraints.

- Which commercial structure is easiest for the bank to understand?
- What license, subscription, activation, or transaction model would be easiest to evaluate?
- What setup-cost range would require additional approval?
- What annual-cost range would require additional approval?
- Would the bank charge employers per successful recipient transaction?
- What rural-bank retained economics would make participation rational?
- What does the bank believe employers would tolerate or value?
- What payback period or business case would the bank need?
- What customer disclosures would the bank expect?

Related assumptions: `LIC-004`, `LIC-005`, `PRC-001`, `RB-001`, `COL-001`.

## Infrastructure

- Is the bank willing to use a bank-owned cloud account?
- Is the bank willing to contract with approved DevOps providers?
- What internal IT capability exists?
- Who should own domains, DNS, certificates, backups, credentials, and cloud billing?
- What incident and support expectations does the bank have?
- What handover or provider-replacement requirements would be required?

Related assumptions: `OPS-001`, `OPS-002`, `OPS-003`, `OPS-004`, `OPS-005`, `CLD-001`.

## Public Value And Institutional Readiness

- Would the offering improve access for employer payroll clients?
- Would it reduce manual work for the bank or employers?
- Would it improve evidence, reconciliation, or complaint handling?
- Would it strengthen the bank's customer relationship?
- What indicators would the bank consider meaningful?

Related assumptions: `PUB-001`, `PUB-003`, `PUB-004`, `INV-003`.
