## Executive Summary

The public-interest case starts with completed payroll outcomes, employer efficiency, recipient flexibility, transparency, evidence, and accountability. Commercial sustainability matters because these outcomes require a system that participating institutions can afford to operate.

## Your Role

The public-interest reader evaluates whether Payroll Starter separates customer funds from revenue, makes roles visible, supports accountable delivery, and avoids hiding unresolved legal, tax, operational, or provider questions.

## Revenue Sources

There is no public-interest revenue line.

The financial model is relevant because sustainable economics can support:

- employer service continuity;
- transparent fee flows;
- rural-bank capability;
- provider replaceability;
- documented evidence;
- repeatable modernization.

## What The Numbers Mean

The model does not claim public value in pesos. It shows whether the commercial structure could sustain the operational activity needed to produce public-interest outcomes.

The Base case becomes positive after the early period before blocked items. Conservative remains weak. That matters because fragile economics can undermine continuity, support, and accountability.

## What Is Still Missing

- `PUB-002`: recipient satisfaction indicator.
- `PUB-003`: employer administrative-burden reduction.
- `PUB-004`: payroll outcome completion indicator.
- `RB-002`: Rural Bank internal support cost.
- `TAX-001`, `NET-001`, and legal/accounting treatment.
- Pilot evidence on complaints, exceptions, support tickets, reconciliation, and delivery success.

## Risks

- Commercial weakness could reduce service continuity.
- Customer funds or payroll value could be misunderstood as revenue if governance is ignored.
- Recipient outcomes may be asserted before evidence exists.
- Privacy, consent, SMS, and data-sharing questions remain unresolved.
- Conservative conditions remain difficult.

## Opportunities

- Make customer-fund separation explicit.
- Improve employer payroll evidence and reconciliation.
- Increase transparency into provider costs and stakeholder roles.
- Build a repeatable rural-bank modernization path.
- Measure public-interest indicators without forcing artificial peso values.

## Key Decisions

- Which public-interest indicators should be measured in the pilot?
- What disclosures should employers and recipients receive?
- How should complaints, failed payments, and exceptions be recorded?
- What evidence is needed before making public claims?
- Which safeguards are needed for privacy, consent, and data sharing?

## Questions This Stakeholder Should Ask

1. Are payroll funds clearly separated from operating revenue?
2. Can employers and recipients understand who is responsible for what?
3. What evidence proves payroll outcomes were completed?
4. How are failures, complaints, and disputes visible?
5. Which public-interest indicators should remain non-financial?
6. Does the commercial model support long-term continuity rather than short-term launch activity?
