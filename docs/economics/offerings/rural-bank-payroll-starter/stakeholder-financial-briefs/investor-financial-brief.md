> Stakeholder revenue figures are entity-level views and include internal ecosystem transfers. They are not additive. Consolidated External Revenue counts external customer inflows once and eliminates internal transfers.

## Executive Summary

The investor should care because the Level 1 model shows an emerging commercial system, not merely a spreadsheet. The important signal is traceability: every material number points back to a governed assumption, provisional input, role, and blocked dependency.

This is not yet an investment projection. It is a disciplined way to learn which assumptions drive value.

## Your Role

The investor is a financing and ownership reader, not an operational waterfall participant. Investor capital is financing, not operating revenue, and no investor return is calculated in the Level 1 model.

## Revenue Sources

The investor does not receive operating revenue in this model.

Investor relevance comes from observing:

- consolidated external revenue trajectory;
- stakeholder contribution before blocked items;
- employer relationship growth;
- platform repeatability;
- evidence maturity;
- unresolved risk.

## What The Numbers Mean

The model suggests a commercial path where employer-level recurring revenue is more important than transaction fees alone. It also shows that Payroll should be evaluated as an incremental offering on shared infrastructure, not as the sole justification for a rural-bank-owned digital platform.

For investment review, the strongest signal is not the current pesos. The strongest signal is that assumptions are now visible, testable, and replaceable with evidence.

## What Is Still Missing

- `RB-002`: true Rural Bank internal support cost.
- `ROY-001`: 3neti royalty or license economics.
- `NET-001`: NetBank or infrastructure fee.
- `TAX-001`: tax and withholding treatment.
- `RISK-002`: non-collection evidence.
- Pilot adoption, retention, completion, support, and cost evidence.
- Investor instrument, ownership, conversion, liquidity, and return assumptions.

## Risks

- The Base and Accelerated paths depend on adoption and employer density.
- Conservative conditions remain commercially difficult.
- Blocked costs may reduce apparent contribution.
- Legal, accounting, tax, and NetBank outcomes may change entity-level economics.
- Placeholder values may not survive provider quotes or pilot data.

## Opportunities

- The model can be improved progressively by replacing placeholders with evidence.
- Payroll creates a first repeatable commercial unit.
- Shared platform economics can improve if additional offerings use the same bank-owned infrastructure.
- Governance discipline can reduce future diligence friction.

## Key Decisions

- Which assumptions must be evidenced before valuation work?
- Which outputs remain too provisional for investor materials?
- Which additional offerings are needed to understand portfolio economics?
- What financing instrument, rights, and return mechanism would be evaluated later?
