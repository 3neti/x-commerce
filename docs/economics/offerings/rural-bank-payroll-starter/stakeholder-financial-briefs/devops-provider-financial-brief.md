> Stakeholder revenue figures are entity-level views and include internal ecosystem transfers. They are not additive. Consolidated External Revenue counts external customer inflows once and eliminates internal transfers.

## Executive Summary

DevOps appears commercially promising in the Level 1 model because setup and recurring managed-operations revenue scale with active banks. That conclusion is still incomplete because setup direct cost, overhead, staffing, tax, and escalation burden require evidence.

## Your Role

The DevOps Provider operates rural-bank-owned environments under delegated authority. The Rural Bank owns the cloud account, domains, DNS, certificates, databases, backups, credentials, cloud billing, and business data.

## Revenue Sources

DevOps revenue comes from:

- deployment setup revenue;
- recurring managed-operations revenue.

Cloud cost remains outside DevOps revenue under the baseline because the Rural Bank owns and pays the cloud account.

## What The Numbers Mean

The recurring managed-operations layer can become meaningful as active banks grow. DevOps contribution is positive in all scenarios before unresolved setup direct cost, overhead, tax, and escalation burden.

The economic logic depends on disciplined scope: the DevOps Provider operates, while the Rural Bank owns.

## What Is Still Missing

- `OPS-003`: direct engineering and tooling cost evidence.
- Setup direct cost evidence.
- Staffing capacity and on-call model.
- Tooling, monitoring, backup, incident, and escalation cost evidence.
- Tax and contracting treatment.
- Cloud cost evidence under `CLD-001`, even though cloud remains a Rural Bank outflow.

## Risks

- On-call and incident burden may be higher than the placeholder model.
- Setup direct cost is not yet fully represented.
- Tooling or support scope may expand without matching revenue.
- Bank-owned infrastructure may require handover and governance work not captured in recurring cost.
- Active bank counts may lag.

## Opportunities

- Build a repeatable approved-provider operating model.
- Standardize deployment and handover.
- Improve margins through reusable tooling, monitoring, and documentation.
- Support multiple offerings on the same rural-bank-owned infrastructure.

## Key Decisions

- What is included in managed operations?
- What support levels are offered?
- How much setup effort is required per bank?
- Which costs are direct DevOps costs versus external cloud costs?
- What evidence is sufficient to replace the Level 1 placeholders?

## Questions This Stakeholder Should Ask

1. What is the real direct cost per active bank-month?
2. How many environments can one delivery team support?
3. What setup cost is currently missing from the contribution view?
4. Which tooling costs scale with banks?
5. What are the incident, backup, and recovery obligations?
6. How does the provider remain replaceable while still earning a sustainable margin?
