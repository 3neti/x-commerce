## Executive Summary

For RBAP, Payroll Starter is a modernization pattern for member banks. It is not just a payroll product. The Level 1 model shows how member banks could adopt a repeatable digital-service capability, but it also shows that Payroll alone should not be expected to carry the full modernization cost.

## Your Role

RBAP is a program and member-bank ecosystem reader. It does not receive operating revenue in the Level 1 Payroll Starter model. Its interest is whether the model can help member banks modernize sustainably, serve employers, and launch further offerings.

## Revenue Sources

RBAP revenue is not modeled.

Program value comes from:

- member-bank capability development;
- repeatable adoption pattern;
- employer-service modernization;
- transparent economics;
- shared evidence acquisition;
- portfolio expansion potential.

## What The Numbers Mean

Payroll can support a modernization rollout only if it is understood as part of a larger bank platform. The current model makes that visible: incremental economics can become positive, while full-cost stand-alone Payroll remains negative.

For RBAP, the lesson is portfolio design. Member banks need a path from one offering to several offerings on the same infrastructure.

## What Is Still Missing

- Member-bank adoption evidence for `ADP-001`, `ADP-002`, and `ADP-003`.
- Employer portfolio evidence for `CUS-001`.
- Rural Bank internal support cost under `RB-002`.
- NetBank, tax, and legal characterization.
- Pilot evidence showing actual operational burden and employer uptake.

## Risks

- Member banks may adopt the platform slower than expected.
- Employer density may be too low at some banks.
- Modernization costs may be misunderstood as Payroll-only costs.
- Conservative member-bank conditions remain weak.
- A one-off deployment approach could weaken repeatability.

## Opportunities

- Build a repeatable rural-bank modernization playbook.
- Use Payroll as the first offering, then add remittance, collections, government payouts, reimbursements, incentives, and other Pay Code-supported services.
- Improve member-bank employer services.
- Gather shared evidence that improves future pricing and support models.

## Key Decisions

- Which member-bank segments are most ready?
- Which banks should pilot first?
- Which additional offerings should be modeled next?
- What evidence should RBAP coordinate across member banks?
- How should member banks evaluate modernization across a portfolio rather than one offering?

## Questions This Stakeholder Should Ask

1. What happens if our member banks adopt this?
2. Which member banks can realistically support employer payroll customers?
3. How do we prevent one offering from carrying the entire modernization case?
4. What evidence can RBAP help collect once across many banks?
5. Which offering should follow Payroll Starter?
6. What governance is needed so member-bank economics remain transparent?
