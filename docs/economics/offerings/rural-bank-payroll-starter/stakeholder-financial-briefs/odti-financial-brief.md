## Executive Summary

ODTI's Payroll Starter economics come from a layered operator model: platform access, employer-level service, and transaction activity. The Level 1 model suggests recurring employer relationships are more important than recipient transaction fees alone.

ODTI results remain Pre-Tax, Pre-Royalty, and NetBank-Fee-Blocked.

## Your Role

ODTI is the commercial operator and platform provider. It receives activation, subscription, employer-service, and transaction-platform revenue while bearing implementation, support, commercial administration, and related operating costs.

## Revenue Sources

ODTI revenue comes from:

- rural-bank platform activation;
- annual platform subscription;
- ODTI share of employer onboarding;
- ODTI share of employer monthly payroll service;
- ODTI recipient transaction-platform revenue.

No 3neti royalty is shown because `ROY-001` remains blocked.

## What The Numbers Mean

ODTI can become viable when platform access and employer-service economics are included. Recipient transaction fees are useful, but they are not the main revenue engine.

Support scale matters. The model already warns that the Base candidate has four employers per active bank, below the ODTI employer support break-even before activation and platform access revenue.

## What Is Still Missing

- `ROY-001`: 3neti royalty or license basis.
- `TAX-001`: tax and withholding treatment.
- `NET-001`: NetBank or infrastructure fee where it affects ODTI economics.
- `ODTI-001`: support cost evidence.
- `ODTI-002`: implementation cost evidence.
- Legal and accounting review for revenue recognition, gross/net presentation, and related-party treatment.

## Risks

- Support costs may scale with employers, incidents, offerings, or banks more than the current placeholder structure shows.
- Royalty treatment may materially change entity-level economics.
- NetBank fees may reduce transaction economics.
- Active bank and employer counts may lag the candidate model.
- ODTI may over-rely on platform access revenue if employer-level adoption is weak.

## Opportunities

- Make employer monthly service the recurring commercial center of Payroll Starter.
- Standardize implementation to improve activation economics.
- Use Payroll as the first commercial proof point for repeatable rural-bank offerings.
- Convert support evidence into better pricing and staffing decisions.

## Key Decisions

- How should ODTI support costs scale?
- What revenue split with Rural Banks is sustainable?
- What is the 3neti royalty or license basis?
- Which costs belong to Payroll versus the shared platform?
- What evidence is needed before moving from Level 1 placeholders to evidence-supported planning?
