## Questions For Reviewers

Reviewers should answer:

- Are the adoption scenarios credible for a second modeled offering?
- Are sponsor counts and batch sizes plausible?
- Is the recurring sponsor-service fee the right commercial unit?
- Is the recipient-disbursement fee secondary or primary?
- What should the Rural Bank retain?
- Which costs belong to Disbursement versus shared platform modernization?
- Should optional notification be included in the first Level 1 model?
- Which assumptions are too weak to authorize even as placeholders?
- Which assumptions require external evidence before any numeric model?
