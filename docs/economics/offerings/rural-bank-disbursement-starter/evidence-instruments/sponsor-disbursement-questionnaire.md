# Sponsor Disbursement Questionnaire

## Purpose

Collect sponsor evidence for disbursement activity, recipient count, pass-through funding, pricing tolerance, reporting, and reconciliation needs.

## Relevant Assumptions

- `DSP-CUS-001`;
- `DSP-CUS-002`;
- `DSP-CUS-003`;
- `DSP-VOL-002`;
- `DSP-PRICE-001`;
- `DSP-PRICE-002`;
- `DSP-PRICE-003`;
- `DSP-FUND-001`;
- `DSP-EXC-001`;
- `DSP-ATT-001`.

## Sponsor Profile

- organization type;
- payout use cases;
- locations;
- current bank or payment channels;
- current disbursement frequency;
- current staff involved.

## Current Workflow

- approval steps;
- recipient list preparation;
- funding process;
- recipient questions;
- failed or corrected payout handling;
- reporting;
- reconciliation;
- evidence requirements.

## Disbursement Activity

- batches per month;
- recipients per batch;
- supplemental or irregular disbursements;
- failed, reversed, corrected, or pending events;
- average funding value, if shareable;
- seasonality or program timing.

## Commercial Willingness

- willingness to pay onboarding fee;
- willingness to pay recurring service fee;
- willingness to pay per-recipient fee;
- willingness to pay optional notification fee;
- preferred billing frequency;
- payment and collection timing constraints.

## Outcome Value

- administrative burden reduced;
- evidence required;
- recipient access improvement;
- reporting and audit value;
- cost of current process.
