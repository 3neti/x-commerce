# Rural Bank And RBAP Disbursement Questionnaire

## Purpose

Collect evidence for rural-bank adoption, sponsor portfolio, readiness, retained economics, and bank support requirements for `OFR-RB-DISBURSEMENT-STARTER`.

## Relevant Assumptions

- `ADP-002`;
- `ADP-003`;
- `DSP-CUS-001`;
- `DSP-RB-001`;
- `DSP-RB-002`;
- `OPS-*`;
- `CLD-001`.

## Respondent Context

- institution or association;
- respondent role;
- bank size or member segment;
- current digital capabilities;
- current payout, aid, benefits, reimbursement, or institutional disbursement services;
- current deployment and support model.

## Sponsor Portfolio

- number of current institutional payout or disbursement customers;
- sponsor types;
- common payout use cases;
- expected sponsors per active bank;
- sponsor onboarding constraints;
- recurring sponsor support requirements.

## Adoption Readiness

- willingness to pilot;
- expected approval process;
- technical readiness;
- compliance readiness;
- staffing readiness;
- likely go-live timing;
- reasons banks may remain inactive after onboarding.

## Commercial Appetite

- preferred sponsor-facing fee model;
- preferred bank retained economics;
- willingness to collect sponsor fees;
- support-cost concerns;
- modernization-cost allocation concerns.

## Evidence Attachments

- anonymized sponsor portfolio data;
- current disbursement service descriptions;
- sample payout reports;
- readiness checklist;
- operations staffing notes.
