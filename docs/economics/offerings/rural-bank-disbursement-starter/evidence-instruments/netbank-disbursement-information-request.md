# NetBank Disbursement Information Request

## Purpose

Obtain role, fee, rail, settlement, API, event-status, and completion evidence for Disbursement Starter.

## Relevant Assumptions

- `NET-001`;
- `NET-002`;
- `DSP-VOL-002`.

## Proposed Role

NetBank or the banking partner should identify whether it would act as:

- account provider;
- API provider;
- settlement participant;
- rail participant;
- transaction-services provider;
- reconciliation participant;
- another regulated role.

## Commercial Information

- setup or onboarding fees;
- recurring account or platform fees;
- per-transaction fees;
- attempted versus successful billing;
- settlement charges;
- reconciliation fees;
- support fees;
- reserve or balance requirements.

## Operational Information

- transaction statuses;
- success, failure, pending, reversal, and settlement definitions;
- reporting cadence;
- exception handling;
- implementation timeline;
- service levels;
- operating limits.

## Legal And Regulatory Information

- contracting party;
- required permissions;
- customer-fund and account structure;
- bank responsibilities;
- ODTI responsibilities;
- sponsor disclosures;
- data-sharing and privacy requirements.
