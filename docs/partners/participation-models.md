# Participation Models

## Possible Models

- referral;
- commission;
- advisory;
- success fee;
- implementation partner;
- managed operations provider;
- channel partner;
- institutional partner;
- investor;
- contractor;
- employee or officer role.

## Required Decision Fields

- participant;
- role;
- contracting entity;
- compensation trigger;
- compensation basis;
- duration;
- exclusions;
- conflict review;
- approval authority.
