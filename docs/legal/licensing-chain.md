# Licensing Chain

## Working Premise

```text
3neti
    owns and develops the intellectual property
        |
ODTI
    licenses and commercially operates the technology
        |
Participating rural banks
    obtain licenses through ODTI
        |
Customers consume rural-bank-branded digital services
```

## Required Distinctions

- IP ownership;
- commercial operation;
- software license;
- sublicense;
- implementation;
- hosting;
- transaction revenue;
- marketplace revenue;
- bank-generated revenue.

This chain is a proposed premise, not a signed arrangement.
