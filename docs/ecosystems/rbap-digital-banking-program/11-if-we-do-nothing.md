# If We Do Nothing

## Rural Banks

Rural banks may continue relying on legacy channels or fragmented vendors, potentially losing digital relevance with customers.

## ODTI And 3neti

The commercial chain may remain unclear, delaying licensing, productization, and stakeholder participation.

## RBAP

RBAP may miss an opportunity to frame a reusable digital services program for member banks.

## Customers

Customers may continue facing limited access to digital disbursement, remittance, notification, validation, and transaction services through their rural banks.

## Questions

- What is the measurable cost of delay?
- Which stakeholder loses the most by waiting?
- Which facts are needed to make the "do nothing" case credible?
