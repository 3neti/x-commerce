# Executive Thesis

The proposed program offers rural banks an affordable path to digital transaction services without requiring full core banking replacement.

## Thesis To Develop

Rural banks can participate in digital services by licensing a transaction-outcome platform operated commercially by ODTI and backed by 3neti intellectual property, with NetBank and other regulated or technical partners participating only under approved structures.

## Required Evidence

- rural-bank pain points;
- implementation cost comparison;
- customer demand indicators;
- transaction volume assumptions;
- provider cost assumptions;
- legal dependencies;
- partner responsibilities.
