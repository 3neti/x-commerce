# Risks And Dependencies

## Commercial Risks

- assumptions mistaken for approved pricing;
- adoption slower than scenario assumptions;
- value-added provider costs reduce markup;
- support obligations exceed maintenance fees;
- unclear revenue-share basis.

## Legal And Regulatory Dependencies

- external terminology for "online CASA" and similar labels;
- deposit, electronic money, payment, agency, settlement, and banking characterization;
- NetBank role and regulated rail usage;
- rural-bank customer disclosures.

## Operational Dependencies

- DevOps provider scope and service level;
- bank onboarding capacity;
- incident response responsibilities;
- backups and restoration exercises;
- release support and change management.
