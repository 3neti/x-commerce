# Adoption Scenarios

## Conservative

Slow institutional adoption, lower transaction volume, limited value-added service attachment, higher support burden, and longer sales cycles.

## Base

Moderate adoption among committed rural banks, practical onboarding cadence, balanced transaction growth, and selective value-added service use.

## Accelerated

Faster network uptake, repeatable onboarding, stronger transaction growth, higher value-added service adoption, and improved operating leverage.

## Required Inputs

The next model must assign explicit assumptions for each scenario and identify evidence needed to validate or revise them.
