# Program Opportunity

The opportunity is to package transaction outcomes for rural-bank networks.

## Potential Advantages

- lower entry cost for digital transaction services;
- rural-bank-branded customer experience;
- reusable platform economics;
- value-added services per transaction;
- managed cloud operations for institutions without internal capacity;
- stakeholder-specific revenue participation.

## Validation Needed

- RBAP member interest;
- NetBank role and economics;
- rural-bank willingness to pay;
- customer usage volume;
- DevOps provider capacity;
- legal characterization.
