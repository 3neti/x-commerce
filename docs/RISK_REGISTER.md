# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-001 | Working assumptions are treated as approved prices. | Misleading proposals or stakeholder expectations. | Label all numbers as assumptions until approved. |
| R-002 | Pass-through funds are treated as company revenue. | Incorrect financial projections. | Separate gross transaction value, fees, provider costs, and net revenue. |
| R-003 | Legal terms are used loosely. | Regulatory or contractual mischaracterization. | Hand legal characterization to x-legal. |
| R-004 | Informal partner roles are treated as rights. | Governance and compensation disputes. | Record only as proposals, scenarios, or decision points. |
| R-005 | RBAP, NetBank, ODTI, 3neti, or rural bank approval is implied. | Stakeholder trust and legal risk. | Mark program documents as proposed until accepted. |
| R-006 | Managed cloud accountability is unclear. | Operational support gaps. | Compare direct, resale, and bundled models. |
| R-007 | x-commerce over-implements too early. | Premature abstractions and extraction risk. | Keep code minimal until commercial decisions are approved. |
