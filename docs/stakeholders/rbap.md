# RBAP

## What's In It For Them?

RBAP may help member banks access a practical digital services program and may receive approved program, referral, sponsorship, or institutional participation economics if formally agreed.

## Contribution

Institutional access, program framing, member engagement, feedback, and possible endorsement or coordination if approved.

## Receives

Member-value program, policy narrative, possible participation revenue, and better insight into member digital readiness.

## Costs

Program coordination, governance review, reputation risk, and legal review.

## Risks

Implied endorsement before approval, member dissatisfaction, unclear responsibility, and regulatory sensitivity.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model referral or program participation only after the basis is approved.

## Open Decisions

- Is RBAP a referrer, sponsor, program owner, convenor, or non-commercial participant?
- What approvals are required?
- What language may be used in member communications?
