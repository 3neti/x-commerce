# Stakeholders

Each stakeholder document should answer:

1. Who is this stakeholder?
2. What problem do they face today?
3. What does the program ask them to contribute?
4. What do they receive?
5. What new revenue can they earn?
6. What costs do they incur?
7. What risks do they assume?
8. What responsibilities do they own?
9. What dependencies must be satisfied?
10. What is their projected five-year financial outcome?
11. What happens if they do nothing?
12. What decisions remain unresolved?
13. What evidence would validate the model?
14. What contractual or regulatory work is required?

Every document should include a "What's in it for them?" summary near the beginning.
