# Participating Rural Bank

## What's In It For Them?

The rural bank may offer branded digital transaction services without replacing its core banking system, while earning or retaining revenue from bank-approved services.

## Contribution

License adoption, onboarding effort, customer support, program promotion, operating compliance, and bank-side settlement participation.

## Receives

Digital transaction access, productized services, training, reporting, and possible new revenue streams.

## New Revenue

Possible transaction fees, service fees, merchant fees, remittance fees, customer acquisition, and deposit or relationship value subject to legal review.

## Costs

License, subscription, maintenance, implementation, cloud operations, support, training, marketing, compliance, and settlement charges.

## Risks

Low adoption, support burden, unclear legal characterization, customer complaints, integration delays, and provider dependence.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model license costs, implementation costs, transaction revenue, value-added service economics, support costs, churn, and incremental customer value.

## Open Decisions

- License model.
- Managed cloud model.
- Bank-retained fee schedule.
- Launch use cases.
- Required contracts and disclosures.
