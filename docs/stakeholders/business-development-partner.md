# Business-Development Partner

## What's In It For Them?

Business-development partners may receive referral, commission, advisory, or success-based compensation if their role is formally approved.

## Contribution

Introductions, opportunity qualification, relationship management, proposal support, or sales execution.

## Receives

Potential referral fee, commission, success fee, or retainer under approved terms.

## Costs

Time, relationships, travel, proposal work, and reputation risk.

## Risks

No approved compensation basis, attribution disputes, conflicts, unqualified opportunities, and compliance concerns.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model only after compensation trigger, amount, duration, and contracting party are approved.

## Open Decisions

- Who qualifies as the originating partner?
- What event triggers compensation?
- How long does participation last?
- Are Mike, Dens, Obbie, or Claire in this category?
