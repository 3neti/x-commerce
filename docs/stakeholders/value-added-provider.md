# Value-Added Service Provider

## What's In It For Them?

Providers may earn usage-based revenue when their services are attached to transactions.

## Contribution

SMS, email, KYC, OTP, signature, selfie, face match, location, feedback, survey, document collection, fraud analysis, reporting, archive, reconciliation, or support services.

## Receives

Provider fees, recurring usage, and distribution through rural-bank transaction workflows.

## Costs

Service delivery, API support, compliance, uptime, integration, and account management.

## Risks

Low adoption, integration failure, privacy concerns, pricing pressure, and service-level disputes.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model provider cost separately from ODTI markup and stakeholder revenue share.

## Open Decisions

- Which services are first-party, third-party, or marketplace?
- Which fees are pass-through?
- Which services require legal review?
