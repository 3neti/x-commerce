# 3neti Research And Development OPC

## What's In It For Them?

3neti owns and develops the intellectual property and may receive license consideration, royalties, or other approved returns from ODTI's commercial operation.

## Contribution

IP development, architecture, package stewardship, technical direction, and future approved software.

## Receives

Royalty, license, equity value, strategic control, and reusable commercial architecture.

## Costs

R&D, documentation, governance, legal review, architecture maintenance, and opportunity cost.

## Risks

IP boundaries unclear, ODTI economics collapsed into 3neti economics, premature commitments, and weak traceability of assumptions.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model royalty or license consideration separately from ODTI operating revenue.

## Open Decisions

- Royalty basis and calculation.
- IP license scope.
- Support obligations retained by 3neti, if any.
- Approval process for package interfaces and extraction.
