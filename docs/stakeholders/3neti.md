# 3neti Research And Development OPC

## Stakeholder

3neti Research and Development OPC is the proposed intellectual-property owner and technology steward for the reusable technology underlying the RBAP Digital Banking Program and future ecosystem programs.

This document is a stakeholder business-case draft. It is not evidence that 3neti has approved final license terms, royalty terms, support obligations, ownership allocations, partner rights, or any regulatory characterization.

3neti must be treated as economically distinct from ODTI. ODTI may commercially operate programs based on 3neti technology, but ODTI operating revenue is not the same as 3neti revenue.

## What's In It For Them?

The program is intended to give 3neti a disciplined path for turning reusable technology and intellectual property into durable commercial architecture without collapsing R&D ownership into day-to-day commercial operation.

3neti's opportunity is to build and protect the reusable IP spine while ODTI, rural banks, NetBank, providers, DevOps operators, and partners participate through approved commercial structures.

If the model works, each successful transaction outcome can become evidence that 3neti's technology supports repeatable commercial programs. That evidence may justify licensing, royalties, platform evolution, investor confidence, future ecosystem expansion, and continued R&D.

No royalty, valuation, ecosystem adoption, IP protection outcome, or future extraction path is guaranteed.

## Problem Today

Reusable technology can lose strategic value if the commercial model around it is unclear.

3neti may face constraints such as:

- unclear distinction between IP ownership and ODTI commercial operation;
- premature pressure to implement pricing, checkout, billing, or licensing logic before the grammar is stable;
- weak traceability between assumptions, documents, package boundaries, and future software;
- informal partner expectations that could be mistaken for rights;
- difficulty deciding which concepts belong in x-change, x-commerce, x-legal, ODTI, or applications;
- risk that one ecosystem's needs become hard-coded into reusable package design;
- lack of validated royalty, license, or support economics.

The program is designed to let 3neti learn which commercial concepts deserve first-class treatment before creating software abstractions.

## Why They Would Participate

3neti participates in the business case to evaluate whether its reusable technology can support repeatable commercial programs across multiple ecosystems.

Potential reasons include:

- protect and develop intellectual property;
- establish a clear 3neti-to-ODTI licensing chain;
- create approved royalty or license consideration models;
- preserve package boundaries between x-change, x-commerce, x-legal, and applications;
- transform commercial learning into reusable architecture;
- build documentation evidence for future software decisions;
- support ODTI without becoming indistinguishable from ODTI;
- prepare future ecosystems beyond RBAP;
- avoid premature implementation of commerce primitives before the grammar stabilizes.

These are strategic and commercial hypotheses. They must be validated through program adoption, licensing discipline, operating evidence, legal review, technical stewardship, and traceable decision records.

## Contribution

The program asks 3neti to contribute:

- intellectual property development;
- package stewardship;
- architecture governance;
- technical direction;
- commercial grammar development through x-commerce;
- boundary discipline between x-change, x-commerce, x-legal, ODTI, and applications;
- review of future extraction proposals;
- decision records for major architectural choices;
- coordination with ODTI on license scope, royalty basis, and package evolution;
- support for legal handoffs where product terminology or regulatory characterization touches architecture.

3neti should not accept undefined implementation, support, DevOps, banking, settlement, legal, or commercial operating responsibilities merely because it owns the underlying technology.

## Receives

Depending on approved arrangements, the program is intended to provide 3neti with:

- a defined IP-owner role;
- a licensing relationship with ODTI;
- potential license consideration or royalty economics;
- stronger traceability between technology and commercial use;
- evidence that reusable technology can support repeatable programs;
- commercial architecture that can inform future software primitives;
- protection against accidental collapse of 3neti and ODTI economics;
- a path to support future ecosystems without reinventing the commercial model each time.

3neti receives an IP and architecture opportunity, not a guarantee of royalty revenue, valuation, adoption, or future software extraction.

## Competitive Position

If the program succeeds, 3neti improves its strategic position as the creator and steward of reusable transaction and commercial architecture for digital banking ecosystems.

The intended positioning benefits include:

- owning and developing the technology foundation rather than operating every commercial relationship directly;
- creating a reusable IP base that can support multiple ecosystems;
- demonstrating that x-change execution and x-commerce commercial architecture can remain complementary;
- preserving a clear role for x-legal in legal characterization;
- supporting ODTI as commercial operator without losing IP identity;
- building evidence that future software abstractions should emerge from documented commercial practice;
- increasing strategic optionality for licensing, partnerships, investment, and ecosystem expansion.

This is strategic positioning, not a claim of market leadership or final IP valuation. 3neti's actual position depends on adoption, legal documentation, IP protection, ODTI execution, technical quality, governance discipline, and the ability to maintain clear package boundaries.

## Revenue Or Value Drivers

Possible 3neti value drivers include:

- license consideration from ODTI;
- royalty revenue based on approved terms;
- increased strategic value of reusable IP;
- documented commercial architecture that supports future ecosystems;
- stronger package discipline and traceability;
- reduced ambiguity over which entity owns which economics;
- improved investor or partner confidence;
- future approved software primitives informed by real commercial evidence;
- future ecosystem reuse beyond RBAP.

The model must separate:

- ODTI gross revenue;
- ODTI net operating contribution;
- rural-bank retained revenue;
- pass-through customer funds;
- provider costs;
- NetBank settlement or rail charges;
- taxes;
- partner commissions;
- 3neti license consideration;
- 3neti royalties;
- 3neti R&D costs;
- 3neti architecture, documentation, and governance costs.

ODTI revenue must not be treated as 3neti revenue. A royalty basis must be defined before any portion of ODTI commercial activity is represented as 3neti income.

## Success Indicators

Early indicators that the 3neti model is working may include:

- a clear 3neti-ODTI license draft;
- explicit royalty or license-consideration assumptions;
- clean separation of ODTI operating revenue and 3neti IP economics;
- x-commerce documents repeatedly using stable commercial concepts;
- x-change boundaries preserved during commercial analysis;
- x-legal handoffs created instead of local legal conclusions;
- no premature pricing, billing, catalog, or checkout abstractions;
- decision records created before major extraction or interface work;
- future ecosystem documents reusing the same grammar;
- ODTI commercial evidence showing the technology can support repeatable programs;
- traceability from assumptions to decisions, documents, and future tests.

These are leading indicators. They are not substitutes for royalty collection, profitability, valuation, or legal enforceability.

## Costs

Potential 3neti costs include:

- research and development;
- package stewardship;
- documentation and commercial architecture work;
- legal review and IP documentation;
- architecture governance;
- technical support to ODTI or applications, if approved;
- opportunity cost from delaying premature software implementation;
- future characterization tests and extraction planning;
- review of provider, bank, ODTI, or application assumptions where they affect architecture;
- tax, accounting, or administrative costs tied to licensing or royalty arrangements.

3neti should model R&D and stewardship costs separately from ODTI operating costs.

## Risks

Key risks for 3neti include:

- IP ownership and ODTI commercial operation become confused;
- ODTI economics are mistakenly represented as 3neti economics;
- royalty basis is undefined or disputed;
- support obligations are implied without agreement;
- commercial abstractions are implemented before the grammar stabilizes;
- RBAP-specific assumptions leak into reusable package architecture;
- x-commerce starts owning execution semantics that belong to x-change;
- x-commerce makes legal conclusions that belong to x-legal;
- informal partner participation is mistaken for ownership or entitlement;
- future extraction from x-change occurs without characterization tests and decision records;
- documentation becomes detached from actual operating evidence.

3neti should not treat package availability as authority to implement commercial engines before commercial decisions mature.

## Responsibilities

3neti may own or share responsibility for:

- maintaining reusable technology and intellectual property;
- stewarding package architecture;
- preserving x-change, x-commerce, and x-legal boundaries;
- reviewing future commercial primitive proposals;
- approving or documenting extraction decisions;
- maintaining traceability between assumptions, decisions, package boundaries, and tests;
- defining license scope with ODTI;
- defining royalty or license-consideration basis with ODTI;
- supporting legal handoffs where architecture and terminology affect characterization;
- ensuring future code follows documented commercial architecture rather than inventing local meaning.

3neti should not own ODTI's commercial operating obligations unless a future approved agreement assigns that responsibility.

## Five-Year Outcome

The five-year model should show 3neti's economics separately from ODTI, rural banks, NetBank, providers, DevOps operators, and partners.

Minimum 3neti inputs:

- license consideration from ODTI;
- royalty basis;
- royalty rate or formula, if approved;
- royalty timing and collection assumptions;
- exclusion of pass-through funds from royalty base, unless expressly approved;
- exclusion or treatment of provider costs;
- ODTI bank adoption assumptions;
- ODTI transaction volume assumptions;
- ODTI value-added service assumptions;
- 3neti R&D costs;
- architecture and documentation costs;
- legal and IP costs;
- support obligations retained by 3neti, if any;
- tax treatment;
- reinvestment or reserve assumptions;
- contingency.

Required scenarios:

- Conservative: slower ODTI adoption, lower royalty base, higher stewardship cost.
- Base: moderate program adoption, documented royalty basis, controlled architecture support.
- Accelerated: repeatable program expansion, clearer royalty collection, broader ecosystem reuse.

The model should show license consideration, royalty revenue, R&D and stewardship cost, legal and IP cost, support burden, and net contribution. No projection should be presented as guaranteed revenue.

## If They Do Nothing

If 3neti does not define the IP and commercial architecture boundary, it may avoid immediate governance, documentation, legal, and licensing work.

The tradeoff is that reusable technology may be commercialized inconsistently. ODTI economics may drift into 3neti assumptions, partner expectations may become unclear, package boundaries may weaken, and future extraction or implementation work may proceed without a stable grammar.

The "do nothing" case should be evaluated honestly. If the commercial model is not yet validated, delaying software implementation may be rational. Delaying basic IP, licensing, and boundary documentation is riskier because ambiguity compounds as stakeholders multiply.

## Open Decisions

- What is the exact 3neti-ODTI license scope?
- What is the royalty or license-consideration basis?
- Are royalties based on license revenue, subscription revenue, transaction charges, net platform revenue, another approved base, or a combination?
- Which amounts are excluded from royalty calculations?
- What support obligations, if any, does 3neti retain?
- Which commercial concepts should remain documentation-only?
- Which concepts may later become package primitives?
- What characterization tests are required before any x-change extraction?
- What decision record is required before future commercial interfaces are implemented?
- How are informal partner contributions acknowledged without creating ownership rights?
- What external terminology requires x-legal review before use?
