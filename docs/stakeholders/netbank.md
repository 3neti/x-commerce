# NetBank

## What's In It For Them?

NetBank may participate through settlement accounts, regulated rail access, banking services, or other approved roles that support rural-bank digital transactions.

## Contribution

Regulated banking infrastructure or settlement support, subject to agreement and legal review.

## Receives

Possible banking fees, transaction charges, account relationships, network growth, or strategic participation.

## Costs

Operations, compliance, support, risk review, and integration work.

## Risks

Role mischaracterization, regulatory exposure, support burden, and unclear transaction accountability.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model only approved banking or rail fees; do not treat pass-through settlement balances as revenue.

## Open Decisions

- What role is NetBank willing and authorized to perform?
- Which fees are NetBank fees?
- Which disclosures, contracts, and compliance controls are required?
