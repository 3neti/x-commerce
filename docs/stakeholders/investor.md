# Investor

## What's In It For Them?

Investors may evaluate whether the program can produce recurring, transaction-linked, and stakeholder-aligned revenue without overstating adoption or ignoring provider costs.

## Contribution

Capital, governance, introductions, or strategic oversight if formally agreed.

## Receives

Potential investment return, exposure to rural-bank digital transaction infrastructure, and commercial data for decision-making.

## Costs

Capital at risk, diligence expense, and opportunity cost.

## Risks

Unapproved pricing, slow adoption, legal dependencies, weak unit economics, partner disputes, and support cost overruns.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model investor return only after investment instrument, ownership, repayment, dividend, or revenue-share structure is approved.

## Open Decisions

- Is investor participation equity, debt, revenue share, convertible, or strategic?
- Which assumptions support valuation?
- Which risks must be disclosed?
