# ODTI

## What's In It For Them?

ODTI may commercially operate the technology licensed from 3neti, contract with rural banks, manage program economics, and build recurring revenue from licensing, subscriptions, transactions, value-added services, and managed operations.

## Contribution

Commercial operation, sales, contracting, support coordination, partner management, and program execution.

## Receives

License, subscription, transaction, markup, service, and possible managed operations revenue, net of provider costs, royalties, commissions, taxes, and operating expenses.

## Costs

3neti royalties, DevOps provider costs, value-added provider costs, support, sales, administration, taxes, bad debt, and contingency.

## Risks

Overstated revenue, provider-cost leakage, unclear accountability, insufficient support margin, and legal dependence.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model gross revenue, provider costs, gross margin, operating costs, royalties, commissions, and net contribution.

## Open Decisions

- Contracting structure with rural banks.
- 3neti royalty basis.
- Managed cloud resale or bundle.
- Partner compensation.
