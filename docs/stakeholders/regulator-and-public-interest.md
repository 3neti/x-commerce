# Regulator And Public Interest

## What's In It For Them?

Regulators and public-sector institutions may benefit from clearer evidence, traceability, consumer protection analysis, and rural financial inclusion if the model is legally sound and operationally responsible.

## Contribution

Policy guidance, approvals where required, public-sector program alignment, or review.

## Receives

Better documentation, clearer roles, audit evidence, inclusion outcomes, and reduced ambiguity.

## Costs

Review time, supervision, and policy engagement.

## Risks

Consumer harm, misleading labels, unclear deposit or payment characterization, data privacy issues, and operational resilience gaps.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model public-interest outcomes separately from company revenue.

## Open Decisions

- Which legal classifications apply?
- Which disclosures are required?
- Which reporting or audit evidence should be preserved?
