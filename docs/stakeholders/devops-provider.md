# DevOps Provider

## What's In It For Them?

The DevOps provider may earn setup and recurring managed operations revenue from rural-bank deployments.

## Contribution

Cloud provisioning, CI/CD, secrets management, deployments, monitoring, backups, restoration exercises, logging, alerting, patching, certificates, incident response, release support, hardening coordination, disaster recovery procedures, cost monitoring, and capacity planning.

## Receives

Possible setup fee around PHP 50,000 per bank and monthly maintenance around PHP 10,000 per bank, subject to quote and contract.

## Costs

Engineering labor, tooling, cloud management effort, on-call capacity, documentation, and support.

## Risks

Under-scoped service level, unclear incident accountability, margin pressure, unmanaged cloud cost, and security obligations.

## Five-Year Outcome

Model setup revenue, monthly recurring revenue, staff capacity, gross margin, churn, and escalation cost.

## Open Decisions

- Direct bank contract, ODTI resale, or ODTI bundled wholesale?
- Service level model.
- Incident response boundary.
- Security and disaster recovery responsibilities.
